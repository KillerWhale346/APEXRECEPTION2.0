Privacy Policy

This page explains, in plain language, how Apex Reception handles information from calls, website visitors and clients. It is not legal advice and you should adapt it with your own legal counsel as your product evolves.

1. Who we are

Apex Reception (“we”, “us”, “our”) provides AI-powered reception and call handling services for businesses. If you have any questions about this policy, you can contact us at contact@apexreception.io.

2. What information we collect

The information we collect depends on how you interact with us:

  • Website forms: name, email, company, phone number and any details you choose to send.
  • Service usage: call metadata (time, duration, number dialed), conversation content and booking details, depending on your configuration.
  • Technical data: basic analytics such as browser type, pages visited and approximate region.

3. How we use information

  • To provide and improve our AI reception service.
  • To respond to demo requests and support questions.
  • To analyse usage in aggregate so we can improve reliability and performance.
  • To meet legal, security or compliance requirements where applicable.

4. How we share information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share limited information with:

  • Service providers that help us run our infrastructure (e.g. telephony, hosting, analytics).
  • Authorities if we are legally required to do so.
  • Your own tools (such as CRMs or calendars) when you explicitly connect them.

5. Data retention

We retain call data and account information for as long as necessary to provide the service or as required by law. You can request deletion of certain data by contacting us.

6. Your choices

  • You can ask us to update or correct your account information.
  • You can request deletion of certain stored data, subject to contractual or legal obligations.
  • You can opt out of non-essential marketing communications at any time.

7. Children

Apex Reception is designed for businesses and is not directed at children under 16.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time as our product or regulations change. When we do, we will adjust the “last updated” date below.
